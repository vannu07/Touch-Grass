The screen is the shortest part.

Last Light writes a ticket for the last twenty minutes of the day: when to leave, which way the sun is, and four things to do with your eyes, ears, and hands. Then it tells you to put the phone away. Open-weight models do the scoring and the copy. Your location never has to leave this machine.
